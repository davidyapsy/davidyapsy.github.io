Connect your Google Sheet, or just try it

Budget Pulse reads a Budget tab and your monthly transaction tabs (Jan, Feb, ...) straight from a Google Sheet you own — nothing is uploaded and no data is stored anywhere but your browser.

First time? Open Settings to enter your OAuth Client ID and Spreadsheet ID — the README walks through getting both in a few minutes.
